# QuickMarkShot User Guide

## 1. Install, permissions, and menu bar

Move `轻截.app` to Applications and launch it. Allow Screen & System Audio Recording on the first screenshot or recording, then quit completely and relaunch. The app appears only in the menu bar. `⌘⇧0` hides or restores the menu bar entry and persists that choice.

## 2. Screenshots

- `⌘⇧1`: capture the main display.
- `⌘⇧2`: drag to select a region, or click a window to capture it.
- Existing images can also be opened from the menu bar menu.

The annotation window opens immediately and preserves the capture's source pixel dimensions.

## 3. Annotation

Choose rectangle, ellipse, arrow, or pen and drag to draw. Select color and line width. Hold Shift while drawing a rectangle or ellipse to constrain it to a square or circle. The toolbar supports undo, redo, clear, copy, and PNG save; Escape closes the window.

## 4. Recording

Press `⌘⇧5` or choose Start Recording, then select:

- Entire display
- Custom region
- Specific window
- Specific application

Enable system audio, cursor, click indicators, and cursor-follow zoom as needed. Application sources include icons. Stop from the floating control at the top right.

## 5. Cursor-follow zoom

Choose 1.5×, 1.8×, or 2.0×. After stopping, QuickMarkShot processes the video locally and attempts to preserve the system-audio track. If zoom processing fails, the raw recording is retained and the reason is shown.

## 6. Output

Recordings use MP4 (H.264 + AAC) and default to `~/Movies/轻截录屏`; Finder reveals the completed file. The screenshot save panel chooses a PNG location.

## 7. Update and remove

Quit QuickMarkShot and replace the old app with a newer release. To uninstall, quit and remove the app. Saved screenshots and recordings are not removed automatically.
